I’m always fascinated with building **photos editor** on the phone, and it’s always hard to do complicated photos/layers manipulation within the little screen.

One of them is to ***group photos/layers and manipulate them at the same time.***

In the PhotoShop, there is a layer panel where you could group items and doing the group update. It’s probably not a good idea to do so on the little screen because:

1.  There is no enough space for putting the layer panel, at the mean while you want to show the preview.
2.  Bring the user to another layer page, that would introduce the cost of going back and forth (also increase the difficulty of engineering).
